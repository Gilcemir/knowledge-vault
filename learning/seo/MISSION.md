# Mission: SEO for MathFlow

## Why
Gil built [MathFlow](https://github.com/Gilcemir/MathFlow) — live at **https://omml2mathml.com/** — a free web tool that converts OMML (Microsoft Word equations) to MathML. He wants two things, equally: people who need that conversion to actually *find* the tool through search engines, and a genuine transferable understanding of SEO he can apply to any future project. He is starting from zero SEO knowledge.

## Success looks like
- Gil can explain how search engines crawl, render, index, and rank a page — and diagnose why a given page isn't ranking.
- omml2mathml.com is technically sound: descriptive titles, meta descriptions, canonical URLs, sitemap, robots.txt, hreflang across PT-BR / ES / EN, and `WebApplication` structured data.
- Google Search Console shows impressions for queries like "converter equação word para mathml" / "convertir ecuación word a mathml" — Portuguese and Spanish searchers are the priority audience.
- Gil can run a basic SEO audit on any site and prioritize fixes by impact.

## Constraints
- **~30 minutes per week.** Lessons must be completable in ~15 min, leaving ~15 min for one tiny applied change to the site. One concept + one commit per session; nothing bigger.
- Applied work happens in the MathFlow repo (ASP.NET Core Razor Pages, .NET 9, server-rendered, localized EN/pt-BR/es).
- Zero prior SEO knowledge — assume nothing; define every term on first use.

## Out of scope
- Paid search / Google Ads (SEM).
- Black-hat / manipulative link schemes.
- Content marketing at scale (blogging pipelines) — revisit only after the technical foundation is done.
